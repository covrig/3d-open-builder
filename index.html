      About 3D Open Builder

      Free, open-source 3D model builder for 3D printing. Everything runs in your browser; your files are never uploaded. MIT licence.

      Source code: github.com/covrig/3d-open-builder

      Libraries and fonts

      Bundled with the app from copies kept in its own repository; nothing is loaded from other sites. Each keeps its own licence (click it to read the text); all are listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.

      LibraryVersionLicence

      Privacy

      3D Open Builder runs entirely in your web browser.

      • Your files stay on your computer. Files you open are read by your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. Exporting creates the file on your computer.
      • No tracking. There are no analytics, cookies, ads or accounts.
      • Your work and settings stay in your browser. The autosaved drawing, the copied objects and your choices (units, colours, snapping, panel order) are kept in this browser's storage so they are there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

      The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request information such as your IP address when your browser downloads the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. The source code is open: github.com/covrig/3d-open-builder.

      Check and Repair

      Repair

      Fixes the mesh problems a slicer trips over: removes broken and duplicate triangles, turns inside-out faces to point outward, closes holes in the surface and merges parts that overlap, so each object becomes one closed solid. Walls, overhangs and size are not changed. Undo takes the repair back.
