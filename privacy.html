Privacy

3D Open Builder runs entirely in your web browser.

  • Your files stay on your computer. Files you open are read by your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. Saving creates the file on your computer.
  • No tracking. There are no analytics, cookies, ads or accounts.
  • Your work and settings stay in your browser. The autosaved drawing, the copied objects and your choices (units, colours, snapping, panel order) are kept in this browser's storage so they are there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request information such as your IP address when your browser downloads the page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The source code is open: github.com/covrig/3d-open-builder.
